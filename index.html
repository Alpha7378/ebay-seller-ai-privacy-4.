eBay Seller AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

eBay Seller AI is a personal application designed to help manage eBay selling activities, including inventory, listings and sales information.

Information We Access

The application may access information from the user's eBay account that is required to provide its selling and inventory management features.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed through eBay is used only to provide the features of the eBay Seller AI application and is not sold to third parties.

Data Security

Authentication credentials and access tokens are intended to be stored securely and are not intentionally shared with third parties.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to operate the application and manage the user's eBay selling activities.

Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the application owner through the associated GitHub account.
